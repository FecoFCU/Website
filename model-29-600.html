About the 29-600

The Beck 29-600 is a Group 29 linear actuator for globe and other linear control valves. It gives accurate, stable valve positioning under continuous modulation, with high thrust to keep the valve tightly seated.

Typical applications include:

  • Feedwater, spray water and steam control valves
  • Fuel and gas control valves
  • Replacing pneumatic actuators where air quality or supply is a problem
  • Upgrading obsolete or unreliable valve drives

IPT Engineering is the UK agent for Beck. We supply new actuators, replace obsolete or unreliable drives with Beck equivalents, and support existing installations with spares and help finding the right manual from the serial number. See our power station and glass furnace case studies for Beck drives still working after 18 years and more.

We are available to help with this product selection, including on-site application reviews, or hands-on demonstrations. If you have any questions about this product please contact us.
