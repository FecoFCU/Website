Electric Actuators

Precise, continuous modulation for demanding industrial applications

For over 80 years, Beck have provided expertise in actuation and automatic process control. Beck electric actuators are designed for precise, reliable control across a wide range of industrial applications.

With a wide range of drive combinations available, Beck actuators can be configured for many different applications. Their robust construction and control capabilities make them suitable for demanding continuous-modulating duties.

IPT Engineering supports the Beck actuator range in the UK and Europe, providing assistance from initial enquiry and application review through to supply, installation and commissioning.

Whether you are specifying a new actuator or looking to replace an existing unit, we can help identify the appropriate Beck drive and mounting arrangement.

Beck Actuator Technology

Beck drives incorporate a durable motor and high-efficiency spur gear train designed for continuous modulation. The design allows the actuator to operate accurately without the conventional duty limitations associated with many motorised drives.

The control element is provided by a factory-calibrated digital control module (DCM). Position feedback is provided by a Contactless Position Sensor (CPS), which is coupled directly to the gear train.

Because the position sensor is contactless, there are no mechanical contacts to wear and the system provides highly precise position feedback over the operating life of the actuator.

The modular design also allows for straightforward replacement and retrofit applications. IPT Engineering can assist with actuator selection, mounting arrangements and application requirements.

Beck actuators are lubricated for life and are designed to require minimal routine maintenance in most applications.

Typical Applications

Beck actuators are used across a wide range of industrial control applications, including damper drives, burner tilts, variable speed couplings, butterfly valves, windbox dampers, gate dampers, high-torque dampers, ID fans, FD fans, PA fans, secondary air dampers, burner registers, overfire air ports, rotary valves, diverter gates, globe valves, diaphragm valves and other long-stroke linear applications.
