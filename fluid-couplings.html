Fluid Couplings

Independent Application & Technical Support

With over 27 years of experience of specifying, installing and maintaining fluid couplings on many sites around the world, we are ideally placed to help you.

We are able to supply spares, repairs, new couplings, and product support for most types of fluid coupling, including those that were supplied by Fluidrive, Vulcan Sinclair, Pembril and SIME.

Being completely independent of any specific manufacturer, we are able to offer totally impartial advice on your requirement, including application and technical support.

All our spares and new couplings are genuine manufactured products, and in most cases they are maintained on an ex-stock basis for rapid dispatch.

Our services include:

  • Application Reviews
  • Control System Upgrades
  • Energy Saving Reviews
  • Maintenance Schedule Writing
  • Installation Reviews
  • Specification Writing
  • On Site Training
