Independent Fluid Coupling Support

Practical assistance for existing and new installations

Fluid couplings are often long-life components, and many installations remain in service for decades. When original equipment becomes difficult to identify or replacement parts are no longer readily available, obtaining the correct information can become as important as obtaining the part itself.

IPT Engineering can help identify existing equipment from model numbers, photographs, drawings and application information. We can then investigate suitable spares, repair options or replacement equipment.

Our experience covers a wide range of industrial applications and coupling arrangements, allowing us to consider the complete drive system rather than just the coupling in isolation.

This is particularly useful when supporting older installations or equipment where the original manufacturer, model or specification is no longer readily available.

Large Variable Speed Fluid Couplings

Scoop-controlled couplings on boiler feed pumps

Our experience is not limited to fixed-fill couplings on conveyors and crushers. IPT Engineering has hands-on experience of large Fluidrive scoop-controlled variable speed couplings, including the set-up and recommissioning of a unit driving a power station boiler feed pump.

In a scoop-controlled coupling, a movable scoop tube sets how much oil stays in the working circuit. Moving the scoop changes the fill, and with it the output speed, giving smooth, stepless control of a large pump or fan from a constant-speed motor. Many of these units are combined with gearing in a single casing, as in the photograph.

Only four of these units remain in service in the UK, all on start and standby boiler feed pumps at nuclear power stations. They are critical, long-life machines, and IPT can help identify them and source parts, including scoop tube assemblies, seals and other spares. Send us the details or see our SCR coupling parts.
