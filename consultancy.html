Engineering Consultancy Services

Practical engineering support for industrial systems

Our experienced engineers provide practical support to help clients assess, improve and maintain industrial equipment and processes.

From initial assessment and problem definition through to design, implementation and commissioning, we can provide the engineering expertise required to improve process efficiency, reliability and productivity.

Our consultancy service is particularly suited to projects that do not require full-time engineering staff. We can provide focused technical support when it is needed, helping businesses solve problems without the cost of a permanent engineering resource.

We can also assist with existing equipment and installations where performance, reliability or availability has become an issue. Our approach is practical and focused on identifying the problem, understanding the cause and developing a workable solution.

Specialist Engineering Services

IPT can provide engineering support across a range of industrial applications, with particular experience in:

  • Conveyor systems and material handling
  • Fuel route control systems
  • Lubrication systems
  • Filtration and separation systems
  • Industrial flow-control equipment
  • Actuators, valves and dampers

For conveyor systems, for example, we can provide re-engineering solutions for existing installations where operating conditions or production requirements have changed.

We can also help where equipment is obsolete or difficult to replace, identifying suitable replacement components or alternative solutions while considering the requirements of the existing installation.

Root Cause Analysis

Equipment failure can have significant consequences for production, maintenance costs and plant availability. IPT can provide engineering support to investigate failed or unreliable systems and identify the underlying cause.

Using the information available, our engineers can investigate the failure, establish the sequence of events leading to the problem and identify practical corrective action.

Where appropriate, we can also advise on changes to equipment, components, operating procedures or maintenance practices intended to reduce the likelihood of the problem recurring.

Engineering Support When You Need It

Whether you need assistance with a specific engineering problem, an existing installation or a difficult-to-source component, IPT can provide focused technical support without requiring a full-time engineering resource.
