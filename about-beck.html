Beck Electric Actuators

Beck Electric Actuators

Designed for demanding continuous modulation applications

IPT Engineering supplies and supports Beck electric actuators for industrial applications where accurate positioning, continuous modulation and dependable operation are important.

Beck actuators are designed and manufactured in a purpose-built facility in Pennsylvania, USA. Their design differs from conventional electric actuators and is particularly suited to applications requiring continuous modulation.

Beck actuators have been used in industrial applications for many decades, including power generation, waste-to-energy, glass, process industries and other demanding installations.

View Beck Actuators

Why Beck?

Continuous Modulation

Beck no-burnout motors are designed for continuous modulation without the duty limitations commonly associated with conventional actuator motors.

Fast Response

The motor reaches full speed and torque in less than 50 milliseconds and stops within approximately 25 milliseconds.

Accurate Positioning

Contactless position sensing and low-backlash spur gearing provide accurate and repeatable positioning.

Beck Actuator Features

Key design characteristics

Motor & Gear Train

  • No-burnout motors designed for continuous modulation.
  • Full speed and torque in less than 50 milliseconds.
  • Stops within approximately 25 milliseconds.
  • Will not coast or overshoot.
  • Wide-face spur gear construction designed to minimise backlash.
  • Gears are lubricated for life.
  • Minimum step size of 0.10% configurable.

Electronics & Positioning

  • Factory-calibrated digital control electronics.
  • Contactless position sensor with no wearing parts.
  • Position sensor directly coupled to the gear train.
  • No torque switches required.
  • HART and Fieldbus Foundation communication options available.
  • Local handswitch and manual handwheel.

Enclosure

  • Dust-tight, weatherproof NEMA 4X enclosure.
  • IP68 protection to 3 metres for 48 hours.
  • Modular design with separated compartments.
  • Robust drop-in design for installation.
  • Can be mounted in any orientation.
  • Operating temperature range approximately -40°C to 85°C.

Industrial Applications

Beck actuators are suited to applications where reliable positioning and continuous modulation are required.

  • Power generation and waste-to-energy
  • Industrial furnaces and combustion systems
  • Glass manufacturing
  • Process control
  • Valves and dampers
