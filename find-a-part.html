Can't find your part?

Send us the details.

Finding an obsolete or discontinued industrial part can bring a production problem to a standstill. IPT Engineering can help.

Tell us what you are looking for and we will search our worldwide network of manufacturers, distributors and suppliers to identify the part or a suitable replacement.

We can help with:

  • Obsolete and discontinued industrial parts
  • Legacy equipment and components
  • Parts where the original manufacturer is no longer supplying them
  • Difficult-to-source replacement parts
  • Industrial spares and maintenance components
  • Identification of parts from photographs or nameplates
