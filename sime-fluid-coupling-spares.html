SIME Fluid Coupling Spares

Obsolete couplings, parts made to order

SIME fluid couplings are no longer manufactured, but many are still in service. When spares are needed, IPT Engineering can help.

Because the couplings are obsolete, SIME parts are made to order, including fusible plugs.

How It Works

  1. Send us the details. The coupling nameplate, size, photographs, drawings or dimensions all help.
  2. We identify the coupling and the parts required.
  3. We quote, and the parts are made to order.
  4. If replacement is the better option, we can advise on a suitable replacement coupling.

Other Obsolete Parts

SIME couplings are one example of the obsolete and hard-to-find equipment we support. If you need a part that is no longer available, use Find a Part.

Send Us the Coupling Details

Tell us the coupling make and size, whether it is a surface or underground application, and the quantity you need. Photographs of the nameplate help. Send us the details or contact us.
