Driving Plate Kits

FCU, SCR and SFU fluid couplings

The Driving Plate is the sprung steel plate that transmits the power from the motor to the fluid coupling.

The tables below give driving plate dimensions for FCU, SCR and SFU couplings. The plates suit Fluidrive, Vulcan Sinclair and Pembril / Premium couplings of the same size.

All our driving plates are genuine manufactured products and come complete with all bolts and lock nuts.

FCU driving plate kits are supplied from UK stock. For SCR and SFU driving plates, please ask for availability.

All Items are generally carried as Ex-Stock items. Dispatch of goods generally within 24 hours of order placement, subject to account status.

If an emergency dispatch is required please contact the sales office.

For other parts, see all our Fluidrive coupling spares, including multi disc kits and fusible plugs.
