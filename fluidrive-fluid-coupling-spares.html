Fluidrive Fluid Coupling Spares

Spares and support for FCU, SCR and SFU couplings

Fluidrive fluid couplings have been fitted to conveyors, crushers, mills, fans and many other drives for decades, and a large number are still in service. IPT Engineering supplies spares and technical support for Fluidrive FCU, SCR and SFU couplings, and for the Vulcan Sinclair and Pembril couplings that share the same design heritage.

If you have the coupling size and type, the contract and serial number, or a photograph, send us the details and we will identify the parts you need.

The Fluidrive Coupling Range

How a Fluid Coupling Works

Fixed-fill and scoop-controlled couplings

A fluid coupling has no mechanical connection between the motor and the driven machine. The motor turns the impeller, which throws oil outwards and across to the runner. The oil circulates between them as a vortex, driving the runner and the output shaft. Because power is carried by the oil, the motor starts unloaded, shock loads are damped and if the driven machine jams, the coupling slips instead of stalling the motor.

A scoop-controlled coupling (SCR) adds a rotating reservoir and a scoop tube. Moving the scoop transfers oil between the reservoir and the working circuit, so the output speed can be varied while the motor runs at constant speed. Our guide to how fluid couplings work explains fixed-fill, delay-chamber, scoop-filling and scoop-trimming couplings step by step, with diagrams.

How an FCU Coupling Is Built

Knowing the main assemblies makes it easier to identify the part you need. The FCU is a fixed-fill traction coupling, made in a range of sizes covering small drives up to more than 1 MW.

  • Input: a driving boss and a flexible steel diaphragm driving plate, with a spherical centre spigot to keep the coupling running true
  • Fluid coupling: an aluminium alloy impeller, runner and casing, with the runner shaft carried on roller and ball bearings and sealed by a lip seal
  • Output: a semi-flexible multi-disc coupling between the runner shaft and the output half coupling
  • Protection: a fusible plug on all but the smallest sizes

Flexible at both the input and the output, the coupling behaves like a short cardan shaft. It tolerates small misalignment and shares its weight between the driving and driven shafts, so both need to be properly supported.

Fluidrive Spares We Supply

We can supply all Fluidrive spares. The parts most often ordered are:

  • Multi disc units (MDU) and output kits for FCU, SCR and SFU couplings
  • Driving plates and input kits
  • Fusible plugs

We also supply every other part, including:

  • Driving bosses and output half couplings
  • Ball and roller bearings, lip seals, O-rings and joints
  • Diaphragm gland assemblies
  • For SCR couplings: scoop tubes, motor bosses and centre spigots
  • Impellers, runners, casings and shafts

Typical Applications

  • Belt and chain conveyors
  • Crushers, ball mills and hammer mills
  • Mixers, agitators and centrifuges
  • High-inertia fan drives
  • Haulages, winches and cranes
  • Rotary dryers and reciprocating pumps

Identifying Your Coupling

Where to find the contract and serial number

For most spares, the coupling's contract and serial number is the quickest way for us to identify it and the parts it needs. You will find it here:

  • FCU: hard stamped into the casing, on the periphery either side of the casing joint
  • SFU: usually engraved on the output side gland assembly
  • SCR: engraved on the plate at the back of the tundish oil filling assembly

For driving plate kits and fusible plugs, the coupling size and type is usually enough. Also send us, if you can:

  • The coupling size and type, for example 10.5 FCU
  • Photographs of the coupling, the nameplate and the worn part
  • Dimensions of the part, such as the outside and inside diameter, thickness and hole positions of a driving plate or multi disc
  • Whether the drive is a surface or underground application
