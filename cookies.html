Cookies Policy

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to help us understand how visitors use the site and to improve its performance and functionality.

What is a Cookie?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your computer, tablet or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a website to recognise a returning browser and can help with functions such as remembering preferences and understanding how visitors use the site.

How We Use Cookies

IPT Engineering may use cookies and similar technologies for website analytics and to help us understand visitor activity. This information can help us improve the website and make it easier to use.

Analytics information is used to understand general website usage and is not intended to identify individual visitors personally.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers are automatically set to accept cookies. You can change your browser settings to refuse cookies or to notify you when a cookie is being set.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the way some websites function.
