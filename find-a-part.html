Obsolete & Legacy Industrial Parts

Can't find the part you need? Tell us what you are looking for and IPT Engineering will search our worldwide network of manufacturers, distributors and specialist suppliers to find the part or identify a suitable replacement. Also see our ex-stock AUMA gearboxes and Vamein valve seats.

Obsolete doesn't necessarily mean unavailable. Equipment can remain in service for decades after its original components have been discontinued. A part number, manufacturer, photograph, nameplate or equipment details may be enough for us to start the search. For an example, see how we sourced a bespoke rotary airlock for a steel works when no standard replacement would fit.

We can help with obsolete and discontinued industrial parts, legacy equipment, difficult-to-source spares and replacement components.
