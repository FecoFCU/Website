Beck Digital Control Module

Providing Reliable Control

Beck Digital Control Module

Modulating control electronics built into the actuator

The Beck Digital Control Module (DCM) positions the actuator in proportion to a standard 4–20 mA or 1–5 V dc control signal. It receives the signal directly and drives the Beck motor itself, so there are no contactors, relays, reversing starters or contact protection devices to wear or fail.

Working with the Beck motor and the Contactless Position Sensor, the DCM gives fast, precise and repeatable positioning for continuous modulating control, in temperatures from -40°C to 85°C without heaters.

Features

  • On-board modular design, in its own sealed compartment
  • Push-button configuration and calibration, with no special tools
  • HART communication as standard, for configuration and diagnostics from a handheld or asset management system
  • Minimum step size 0.10% of span (0.15% typical), configurable
  • Stall protection with a configurable time to stall from 30 to 300 seconds
  • Configurable action on loss of input signal: stay in place or move to a preset position
  • 4–20 mA position feedback for remote indication
  • Demand input and position feedback isolated from ground and the ac power line
  • Advanced diagnostics
  • No lithium batteries in the electronics

Options

  • Feedback display: a window in the DCM cover shows actuator position as a percentage of travel, to within a tenth of a degree, with green and red LEDs that light at adjustable positions.
  • Remote mounting: on most Beck actuators the DCM can be mounted in a separate enclosure at any convenient distance from the drive, with or without a Handswitch. Ideal for actuators in hot, wet or inaccessible locations.
  • Backup Power Unit: an ultra-capacitor unit, for ordinary and hazardous locations, that provides short-duration power for a fail-to-position response or to keep the actuator working for a short period. Available for drives up to and including the 11-309.

Contactless Position Sensor

Beck’s patented Contactless Position Sensor (CPS) has provided reliable control feedback with infinite resolution in Beck actuators for many years.

  • Coupled directly to the gear train through anti-backlash gears
  • No contacting or wiping surfaces to wear or lose contact intermittently
  • Precise, reliable feedback with infinite resolution
  • Position is never lost on loss of power, and no battery is needed
  • Provides 4–20 mA feedback to the DCM
  • Robust chassis-mount design

Other Control Options

Where a digital positioner is not needed, Beck drives are also available with analogue position control, direct ac control from a remote controller or switches, multi-position control with cam-operated switches, or simple open/close control. All options are available for 120 V ac and for 208, 240, 380, 415, 480 and 575 V ac supplies.

The DCM is fitted to the Group 11 quarter-turn valve actuators and Group 11 rotary damper drives. Contact us to discuss the right control option for your application.
