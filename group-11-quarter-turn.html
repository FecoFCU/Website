Group 11 Quarter Turn

Group 11 Quarter-Turn Valve Actuators

Continuous modulating control of butterfly, ball and plug valves

Precise, reliable and consistent positioning of control valves is critical to overall process control. Beck’s ability to track the control signal quickly, accurately and consistently lets you use the full capability of your control system, reducing process variability, improving product quality and lowering operating costs.

Group 11 valve drives are engineered for direct coupling to quarter-turn ball, plug and butterfly valves. They can also be installed with specially engineered crank arm and linkage arrangements, which give 100° of travel and, with linkage characterisation, higher seating torque on some valve types.

Valves and drives can be ordered together as pre-engineered assemblies ready for drop-in installation, or the drives can be supplied separately with the hardware needed to fit them to existing valves.

Why Group 11 on a Control Valve

  • No duty limit: the Beck no-burnout motor allows true 100% continuous modulation, with no starts-per-hour restriction.
  • Fast, precise positioning: full speed and torque in under 50 ms, stops within 25 ms, with no coasting or overshoot. Minimum step 0.10% of span (0.15% typical).
  • Nothing to trip: starting and stall currents are close to running current, so no thermal overloads or torque switches are needed, only normal short-circuit protection.
  • Spur gearing, not worm gearing: heat-treated alloy steel and ductile iron gears essentially eliminate wear-induced backlash. An integral self-locking mechanism holds at least 200% of rated torque with the motor de-energised.
  • No declutch: the spoke-free handwheel is always engaged. The motor turns at only 72 or 120 rpm, so the handwheel poses no danger, and mechanical stops prevent manual overtravel.
  • No routine maintenance: grease-sealed bearings, a gear train lubricated for life and contactless position sensing. Three year warranty.

Models and Torque

Each size is available direct-mounted for 90° travel or linkage-mounted for 100° travel. Full stroke times range from 10 to 90 seconds per 90° depending on model and torque (on 50 Hz supplies, timings are 20% longer).

General Specification

Input Power
120 V ac single-phase, 50 or 60 Hz, standard.
208, 240, 380, 415, 480 and 575 V ac at 50 or 60 Hz with an integral transformer (380 and 415 V three-phase). Motor current 0.17 to 3.0 A.

Operating Conditions
-40°C to 85°C (-40°F to 185°F), 0 to 100% relative humidity, without heaters.

Enclosure
Precision-machined aluminium alloy castings with corrosion-resistant polyurethane paint. IP68 (3 m for 48 hours) and Type 4X, with separate compartments for the motor, control module, position sensor, gear train and terminals.

Control and Feedback
4–20 mA demand input with 4–20 mA position feedback and HART, using the Digital Control Module. Analogue, direct ac, multi-position and open/close control options are also available.

Isolation
Demand input and position feedback signals are isolated from ground and the ac power line.

Action on Loss of Power
Stays in place. An optional ultra-capacitor Backup Power Unit (models up to 11-309) can drive the valve to a set position.

Action on Loss of Input Signal (Power On)
Stays in place or, on some drives, moves to a field-configurable preset position.

Stall Protection
Configurable time to stall from 30 to 300 seconds with the Digital Control Module.

Limit and Auxiliary Switches
Two over-travel limit switches. Up to four 6 A, 120 V ac auxiliary switches (S1 to S4), cam-operated and field-adjustable.

Handswitch
Permits local electrical operation independent of the controller signal. Standard on all units, with a lockable version available. An optional auxiliary contact can indicate when the Handswitch is out of AUTO.

Certification
CE and UKCA approved. ATEX versions available: Group 11 for Zone 22, and Groups 11E and 11H for Zones 2 and 22.

Cable Entries
Metric cable entry adaptors are supplied for European orders.

Mounting Orientation
Any orientation, with no limitations.
