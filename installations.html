Beck Actuator Installations

Fit and forget, across industry

Beck electric actuators have been in industrial service since 1956, and many of the installations we have supplied and supported are still running years later without maintenance. Below are examples from the sectors we work in, each with the full story.

Typical applications are fan and damper drives, control valves and fluid coupling scoop control, often replacing hydraulic, pneumatic or unreliable electric drives.

Power Stations

Hydraulic ID fan damper drives replaced with Beck actuators that made over 100 million starts in eight years, plus a 24-actuator primary air damper upgrade.

Biomass Conversion

Beck actuators on fan, damper and valve applications at Europe’s largest coal to biomass conversion, and drives recommissioned after damper maintenance.

Glass Furnaces

Gas skid, forehearth and damper actuators, a drop-in gas valve package and complete line rebuilds, with drives still in service after 20 years.

Why Clients Choose Beck

  • Precise, repeatable modulating control, with fine step sizes and low hysteresis
  • Energised only on a change in demand, so low power use and low cable cost
  • No oil, filters or leaks, unlike hydraulic drives
  • Drop-in replacement packages, factory-calibrated where needed
  • Long maintenance-free service, often running between outages without attention
