Industrial Engineering, Equipment & Specialist Parts

IPT Engineering supplies and supports industrial equipment and components for demanding applications.

IPT Engineering supplies and supports industrial equipment and components for process, power, water, waste-to-energy and other demanding industrial applications.

We work with established manufacturers and specialist suppliers to provide equipment, replacement components and technical support — from electric actuators and valves to fluid couplings, industrial dampers and AirSweep material-flow systems.

We are also a specialist sourcing service for obsolete, discontinued and difficult-to-source industrial parts and spares, helping customers locate components that are no longer readily available through normal supply channels.
