Butterfly Valve Size Chart

DN to inches, pipe sizes and valve lengths

Butterfly valves are sized by the nominal diameter (DN) of the pipe they fit, in millimetres, or by the nominal pipe size in inches. DN is a nominal size, not an exact measurement: a DN100 valve fits a 4 inch pipe with an outside diameter of 114.3 mm.

How to Measure for a Replacement Butterfly Valve

  • Read the body: most valves have the DN size, pressure rating and flange standard cast or marked on the body or label
  • Measure the pipe: measure the outside diameter of the pipe and find the nearest value in the chart
  • Count the flange bolts and measure the bolt circle: this identifies the flange standard (PN10, PN16, ANSI 150 or BS 10)
  • Measure face to face: the gap between the pipe flanges, so the new valve fits without moving pipework
  • Note the top flange: if the valve has an actuator or gearbox, note the mounting flange (for example F07 or F10) and shaft size

Send us the details, or a photo of the valve and its label, through the quotation form and we will identify a suitable replacement.

Size Questions

What size is a DN100 butterfly valve in inches?

DN100 is 4 inches. It fits steel pipe with an outside diameter of 114.3 mm.

What is DN in valve sizes?

DN stands for diameter nominal, the European nominal size in millimetres. It is close to, but not the same as, the actual bore or outside diameter of the pipe.

How do I convert DN to inches?

Divide by 25 as a rough guide, then use the standard pairs: DN50 = 2 inch, DN80 = 3 inch, DN100 = 4 inch, DN150 = 6 inch, DN200 = 8 inch, DN250 = 10 inch, DN300 = 12 inch.

What sizes do butterfly valves come in?

Vamein butterfly valves are made from DN50 (2 inch) to DN2000 (80 inch). Sizes up to DN350 are available from stock and typically delivered in about 10 days; larger sizes are available, please ask.
