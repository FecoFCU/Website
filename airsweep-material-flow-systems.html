Do You Have a Blocked Silo?

AirSweep systems can help restore bulk-material flow

One of the common problems with bulk storage silos is bridging, where material builds up inside the vessel and eventually restricts or stops the flow.

IPT Engineering supports AirSweep material flow systems in the UK, providing a solution for difficult bulk-material flow applications.

AirSweep systems are designed to provide an on-demand flow of even difficult materials by helping eliminate bridging, ratholes and material build-up.

Each powerful pulse from an AirSweep nozzle directs a high-pressure, high-volume 360-degree burst of compressed air or gas between the material and vessel wall. This helps lift and sweep stagnant material back into the flow stream.

The patented nozzle design uses a single moving component and is designed to provide an immediate, dust-tight reseal after each pulse, helping prevent clogging and material feedback.

AirSweep systems can be installed on metal, concrete, fibreglass and wood vessels and are externally mounted for straightforward access to the equipment.

Why Use AirSweep?

Designed to keep bulk materials moving

Patented Design

The nozzle design uses only one moving part and is designed to reseal immediately after each pulse, helping prevent clogging and material build-up.

Full Capacity

Each pulse can activate material across a wide area, helping maintain controlled first-in, first-out flow and make better use of available bin capacity.

Reduced Cleaning

AirSweep systems can reduce flushing and cleaning time between product runs. The nozzles are mounted externally for easier access and maintenance.

No Vessel Damage

The system is designed to avoid the vibration, stress and wear associated with some mechanical material-flow devices. It can be mounted to metal, concrete, fibreglass or wood vessels.

Efficient & Durable

Uses plant air as the energy source and is manufactured from high-grade materials for a long service life.

AirSweep Applications

Keeping difficult bulk materials flowing

AirSweep systems have been used with a wide range of difficult bulk materials, from titanium dioxide to food products.

Industries using AirSweep technology include food processing, feed and grain, aggregates and mining, pharmaceutical processing and chemical processing.

The system can be considered where bridging, ratholing or material build-up is causing unreliable discharge from a silo, bin or other bulk storage vessel.

AirSweep can also provide an alternative to other material-flow devices such as:

  • Air Cannons
  • Fluidisers
  • Vibrators
  • Live Bottom Bin Dischargers
