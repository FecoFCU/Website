Industrial Butterfly Valves

UK Distributor for Vamein Valves

IPT Engineering supplies the Vamein range of industrial butterfly valves for on/off and flow-control applications across a wide range of industries.

Vamein butterfly valves are quarter-turn assemblies that can be used either for isolation or as modulating devices to regulate flow.

The range includes wafer, lugged and flanged designs with resilient seating options. Standard valves are available from nominal diameters of 50mm (2") up to 1200mm (48"), while PTFE-seated valves are available in wafer configuration from 50mm (2") up to 300mm (12").

Depending on configuration, the valves are available for working pressures up to 16 bar (230 psi), with operating temperatures ranging from -40°C (-40°F) to 180°C (356°F).

The valve design provides a low pressure drop through the valve, helping to minimise the torque required for operation. Components are manufactured to close tolerances and are designed for interchangeability.

Vamein valves are designed in compliance with API 609, with manufacturing processes certified to ISO 9001:2015. Components are identified with manufacturing code numbers to provide traceability of materials and manufacture.

The valves meet the requirements of the Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU and are available for applications covered by ATEX 2014/34/EU where the appropriate configuration is specified.

Operation can be by lever, handwheel or actuator. IPT can also supply automated valve packages using suitable electric actuators, including the Beck range.

Applications include heavy industry, energy, shipbuilding, water and sewage treatment, chemical and process industries and food production.

Design and Operation

Manual, gearbox or automated

Why a Vamein Valve

  • No gaskets needed: the liner seals against the pipe flanges
  • Low operating torque, so smaller actuators
  • Can be used for regulating flow as well as isolation
  • No pins, screws or keys in the disc, and all parts interchangeable
  • Liners easy to replace
  • Centring holes for quick, accurate installation
  • Body design allows pipe insulation
  • Every body, disc and liner marked for full material traceability

Operation

  • Lever: DN50 to DN300, with lockable regulating positions
  • Gearbox: DN50 to DN1200, IP67, self-locking, with position indicator; recommended from DN250 on standard valves and from DN150 on Galactic
  • Electric actuator: on/off or modulating, including Beck electric actuators for precise control
  • Pneumatic actuator: double-acting or spring-return, with NAMUR solenoid valves, limit switches and positioners
  • The ISO 5211 top flange means a manual valve can be automated later on site, without adaptors

Butterfly Valve Questions

What is the difference between a wafer and a lug butterfly valve?

A wafer valve is clamped between two pipe flanges by long bolts. A lug valve has threaded lugs, so it is bolted to each flange separately and can stay in place when the pipework on one side is removed.

Can a lug butterfly valve be used at the end of a line?

Only occasionally, and not with a cast iron body. Vamein limits end-of-line pressure to 5.6 bar for DN50 to DN150 and 3.5 bar for DN200 to DN600, and the line should be reconnected before normal service.

Which seat material do I need?

It depends on the medium and temperature. EPDM suits water and air up to about 100°C, NBR suits oils and fuels, Viton suits hydrocarbons and higher temperatures up to 180°C, and PTFE (Galactic) suits corrosive chemicals. Tell us the duty and we will confirm the choice.

Do Vamein butterfly valves need flange gaskets?

No. The liner seals directly against the pipe flanges, so no additional gaskets are needed.

Can I fit an actuator to a manual valve later?

Yes. The top flange is to ISO 5211, so a standard electric or pneumatic actuator can be fitted on site in place of the lever or gearbox.

What pressures and sizes are available?

Standard valves run from DN50 (2”) to DN2000 (80”), rated 16 bar up to DN300 and 10 bar above. Higher pressures and other drilling standards are available on request.

Are ATEX valves available?

Yes. Vamein valves can be supplied for use in potentially explosive atmospheres. Tell us the zone when you ask for a quotation.
